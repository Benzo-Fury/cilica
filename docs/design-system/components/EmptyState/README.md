# EmptyState

Empty states explain what will appear here and offer the one action that fills it.

A 64px line drawing in `ink-3` that draws itself once (900ms, staggered strokes), a `title`-style headline, one or two sentences in `ink-2`, a primary action with its shortcut and an optional ghost alternative. No illustrations with people or colour; the delight is the drawing motion.

## Tip

An empty screen may teach one thing: `tip` takes an `EmptyTip` (a short line and its keycaps, `ink-3`, under the actions). Use `ShortcutTip` from `app/keybinds` so the keys follow the user's own bindings. One tip per screen, never a list.

Home shows "a line from your week" in place of its body text when Margin has one (asked for at most once a day).
