# Cilica trademark policy

The Cilica code is open for anyone to use under its licenses. The Cilica **name and logo** are not. They tell people that what they are using comes from the Cilica project, so this policy explains when you can use them and when you can't.

This policy covers the name "Cilica", the Cilica logo and app icon, and any name or logo that looks or sounds confusingly similar (together, the **marks**). The marks belong to Neo Haggard (the **owner**).

The open source licenses (the GNU AGPL v3 for the desktop app, the Cilica Server License for the server) cover the code only. They give no rights in the marks.

## The basic rule

Use the marks in a way that is accurate and does not suggest that the owner made, approved or supports something they didn't.

## What you can do without asking

- **Talk about Cilica.** Write articles, reviews, tutorials, videos, books or courses about Cilica, and use the name to refer to it.
- **Share unchanged copies.** Redistribute official Cilica releases, without changes, under the name Cilica.
- **Say what your work does with Cilica.** Use the name in plain text to describe compatibility, for example "Import tool for Cilica", "Theme for Cilica" or "Works with Cilica". Your own name must come first and be more prominent than "Cilica".
- **Run a community group.** Name a non-commercial user group, meetup or chat channel after Cilica, for example "Cilica users Berlin", as long as it is clear it is not run by the owner.
- **Link to the project.** Use the unchanged logo to link to <https://cilica.app> or the Cilica GitHub repository.
- **Package Cilica for a Linux distribution or package manager,** with only the changes needed for it to build, install and run there, such as paths, dependencies or security fixes.

## What needs written permission

- **Modified versions.** If you change Cilica and share the result, you must give it a different name and icon. See [Forks](#forks).
- **Hosted services.** Any service, free or paid, that uses "Cilica" in its name or marketing to offer sync, storage, accounts or team features, such as "Cilica Cloud", "Cilica Sync" or "Hosted Cilica".
- **Products and company names.** Using a mark, or something similar, in the name of a company, product, plugin store, app or service.
- **Domain names and social accounts** that contain "cilica", unless they are clearly fan or community sites under the rules above.
- **Merchandise.** T-shirts, stickers or other goods with the marks, if you sell them.
- **Changed logos.** Altering, animating, recolouring or combining the logo with other logos.

Ask through <https://cilica.app>. Say what you want to do, and where.

## Forks

You are welcome to fork Cilica under its license. When you share a modified version:

1. choose a name that is not "Cilica" and not confusingly similar to it;
2. replace the logo and app icon with your own;
3. change the app's bundle identifier (`app.cilica.desktop`) and any update or download URLs that point to the owner; and
4. you may say truthfully that your project is "based on Cilica" or "a fork of Cilica", in plain text, without the logo.

You must still keep the copyright and license notices the licenses require. This policy never asks you to remove them.

## Logo rules

When you use the logo under this policy:

- use the official files, without changes to shape, colour or proportions;
- leave clear space around it, and don't place it on a busy background where it is hard to see; and
- don't make it the most prominent element on your page, product or packaging.
