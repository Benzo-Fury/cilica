Cilica mobile design system

Native chrome, Cilica content. iOS uses Liquid Glass and SwiftUI; Android uses Material 3 Expressive and Compose. Both share Cilica's colours, note typography, voice and springs. Read README.md, ios.md and android.md for the rules.

See the key screens

Navigation

  • AppBarTop bars: glass circles on iOS, a Material top app bar on Android.
  • DrawerPlaces, not actions. Opens from ☰ or an edge swipe on the root screen.

Lists

  • NoteRowTwo-line rows under date sections. Swipe for Pin, Move and Delete.
  • SearchFieldiOS: a glass pill in the bottom toolbar. Android: an M3 search bar at the top.

Notes

  • NoteEditorNote fonts inside native chrome. The format bar docks above the keyboard.

Cilica

  • AssistantThreadBubbles for you, plain text for Cilica, tool steps on one line, citations as chips.
  • ComposerHome: empty shows Record, typing morphs it to Send. Notes: a one-line pill.

Recording

  • RecorderBarThe composer's Record button grows into this bar. Stop is the one accent control.
  • RecordingActivityWhat recording looks like outside the app: Live Activity on iOS, an ongoing notification on Android.
  • TranscriptSpeaker colour lives on the avatar ring only. Text stays ink.

Controls

  • ButtonPills on both. One accent button per screen. Android adds the extended FAB.
  • ChipMeta under a note title, and context in the composer.
  • SegmentedControlNotes ⇄ Transcript on a meeting note.

Surfaces

  • MenuLong-press a note or tap More. Destructive actions last.
  • SettingsListGrouped rows with leading icons. Appearance uses three thumbnail cards.
  • SheetCilica over a note, pickers and Settings (iOS).

Feedback

  • DialogOnly for irreversible actions with no Undo.
  • EmptyStateA glyph, a headline, one sentence and one action.
  • ToastPast tense, with one action. iOS: glass pill. Android: M3 snackbar.
